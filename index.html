    How this calendar reads permits

    Rec & Parks permits: repeating bookings such as "every Sunday" are expanded into individual dates, and dates listed as exceptions are left out. Each permit states how many bookings every event has; if the number found here is different, a note appears above the calendar. Only approved permits are shown.

    School-system reservations: every booking is listed under its date. Only confirmed bookings are shown; any others are named in a note. If a listed date has no booking that could be read, a note says so.

    On small screens the calendar lists only the days that have bookings.
