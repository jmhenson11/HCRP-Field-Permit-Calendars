    How this calendar reads permits

    Repeating bookings such as "every Sunday" are expanded into individual dates, and dates listed as exceptions are left out. Each permit states how many bookings every event has; if the number found here is different, a note appears above the calendar. Only approved permits are shown. On small screens the calendar lists only the days that have bookings.
